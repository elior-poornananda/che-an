chean institute

We believe life unfolds from within.

Despite advances in therapy, coaching, spirituality, and artificial intelligence, many individuals remain emotionally fragmented — informed, advised, and assisted, yet internally misaligned and unable to live in accordance with the life they seek to unfold.

Much of today’s support systems are designed to intervene — to explain, correct, diagnose, persuade, or optimize.

Yet inner coherence cannot be installed from the outside. It emerges only when awareness is restored from within.

Che’an exists to study a different premise:

that lasting clarity, healing, and meaningful direction arise not from external authority, but from the restoration of inner coherence.

When reflective presence is held without judgment, prescription, or agenda, individuals may naturally release internal stagnation, regain clarity, and re-enter self-directed living.

The Institute studies how such conditions of presence — when combined with human awareness — allow coherence to re-emerge, rather than be imposed.

Inner-State AI™

Inner-State AI™ is a research concept developed by Che’an to study how emotion-aware, reflective artificial intelligence can support the restoration of inner coherence — without judgment, diagnosis, or directive control.

The Institute explores Inner-State AI™ as a long-term inquiry into how reflective presence, mediated through intelligent systems, may contribute to healing, alignment, and meaningful self-direction across future generations.

Inner-State AI™ is a trademark of Che’an Institute Pte. Ltd.

Che’an is a research and education initiative devoted to the study of inner states, emotional coherence, and human alignment in the age of intelligent systems.

The Institute does not diagnose, prescribe, or instruct. It exists to understand the conditions under which inner coherence naturally returns.

Che’an was initiated by Elior Poornananda, founder and inventor of the Che’an framework and Inner-State AI™.

His work explores the conditions under which inner coherence, emotional clarity, and self-directed living naturally emerge — without prescription, diagnosis, or external authority.
